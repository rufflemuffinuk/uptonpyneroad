About this page

We know a road closure is scheduled on the road from Upton Pyne Cross to Pye Corner until early October.

This is a micro website to track when the road is open and closed based on resident reports.

Based on what we know, the road is expected to be open from 16:00 to 8:30am daily and at weekends, and closed on weekdays while work is underway — until a resident reports otherwise.

Once someone reports it, that report is what's shown, and it stays showing until someone updates it again — it doesn't switch back automatically. If a report starts to look old, the page will flag it and ask for someone to confirm.
